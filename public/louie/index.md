> "Why work hard when you can work smart?"
>
> — the general Louie philosophy

Louie rounds out the trio of Donald Duck's nephews. In the 2017 *DuckTales*
he is the schemer: the one most interested in shortcuts, get-rich-quick
plans, and whether Uncle Scrooge's money might someday trickle down.

## A family of names

The nephews belong to a large, well-documented duck family tree. A few
relatives, for orientation:

| Relative | Relation to Louie |
|----------|-------------------|
| Donald Duck | uncle and guardian |
| Della Duck | mother |
| Scrooge McDuck | great-uncle |
| Daisy Duck | Donald's girlfriend |
| Webby Vanderquack | friend (2017 *DuckTales*) |

## Why three?

Three is the smallest number that makes a crowd. A pair of nephews could
take turns; three can interrupt each other, split up at a fork in the road,
and still leave one behind to get into trouble. Stories use this a lot:

- one nephew distracts Donald
- one consults the Guidebook
- one does the thing they were told not to

The order of the names never changes, even when the order of the ducks
does.
