## From the Money Bin

Uncle Scrooge wants it known that this newsletter cost nothing to produce,
and that he would like that to stay true. The paper is recycled from old
ledgers. The ink is thinned with Duckburg tap water. The staples are
counted.

### A word on the Number One Dime

Visitors to the Money Bin may look at the Number One Dime. Visitors may not
touch, breathe on, photograph, or think too hard about the Number One Dime.
Magica De Spell has been notified that the same rules apply to her.

## Notes from the Guidebook

Every Junior Woodchuck carries the *Junior Woodchucks Guidebook*, which has
an answer for everything except why Uncle Donald won't let anyone else hold
the boat's tiller. This issue's selections:

1. In quicksand, lie flat and stay calm. Then blame Louie.
2. To tell real gold from fool's gold, ask Uncle Scrooge. He'll know by
   smell.
3. When three nephews disagree, the middle one breaks the tie. The middle
   one is [Dewey](./dewey/). Dewey would like this written down.
