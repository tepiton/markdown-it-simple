> Three ducks, one sentence, split three ways.

Hughie (more often spelled *Huey*) is one of Donald Duck's three nephews,
alongside [Dewey](../dewey/) and [Louie](../louie/). For most of their
history the three were close to interchangeable: same height, same caps,
same habit of finishing each other's sentences.

## Origins

The nephews first appeared in a Sunday newspaper strip in October 1937,
drawn by Al Taliaferro. Their animated debut followed in *Donald's Nephews*
(1938), where they arrive for a visit and wreck their uncle's house with a
polo game played on tricycles.

### Who is who

For decades, the colors of their caps and shirts varied from one story to
the next. The arrangement most fans now treat as canon:

| Nephew | Color | Mnemonic |
|--------|-------|----------|
| Huey   | red   | the reddest of the three names |
| Dewey  | blue  | dew is water, water is blue |
| Louie  | green | leaves, as in "Lou-leaf" |

## The Junior Woodchucks

In the comics, the nephews are members of the Junior Woodchucks, a scouting
organization introduced by Carl Barks in 1951. Their *Junior Woodchucks
Guidebook* holds an answer to nearly any problem, which conveniently ends
many a plot.

## Later appearances

- *DuckTales* (1987), where the nephews move in with Scrooge McDuck
- *Quack Pack* (1996), which aged them into teenagers
- *DuckTales* (2017), which gave each nephew a distinct personality and
  voice; Huey became the rule-following planner who carries the Guidebook
